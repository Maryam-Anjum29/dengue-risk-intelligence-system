Key insights
Temperature is the strongest predictor: each 1°C rise above 25°C corresponds to ~6 additional weekly cases. Humidity adds ~3 cases per % increase above 60%.
Clear seasonal pattern — dengue peaks in July–August coinciding with monsoon humidity (80–95%) and temperatures of 30–35°C. January–February are lowest-risk months.
Rainfall shows a moderate positive effect, but with diminishing returns at extreme values. Heavy rains flush breeding sites, tempering the expected spike.
The OLS model explains ~88% of variance (R² ≈ 0.88), confirming that climatic variables alone are strong signals for surveillance and early-warning systems.
